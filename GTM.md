# Go-To-Market Strategy — SpendSmart AI

## Target Customer

**Primary:** Engineering managers and CTOs at startups (5–50 people) who have accumulated multiple AI tool subscriptions without a clear picture of ROI.

**Secondary:** Operations/finance leads at mid-size companies doing a software spend audit.

**Pain point:** "We're paying for Cursor, GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT, and Claude across the team. I don't know if we're overpaying or if people are actually using these tools."

---

## Distribution Channels

### 1. Organic / SEO
Target keywords: "AI tool cost comparison", "is GitHub Copilot worth it", "Cursor vs Copilot pricing", "reduce AI subscription costs". SpendSmart audit pages are publicly shareable — each audit URL is indexed and serves as a long-tail SEO entry point.

### 2. Viral sharing
Every audit result is a unique URL. Users naturally share their results with their team ("here's our AI spend audit") or post them publicly on Twitter/LinkedIn. No explicit referral mechanic needed — the shareable URL is the growth loop.

### 3. Hacker News / Reddit
Post "Show HN: I built a free AI spend auditor" and "We audited our AI tool spending — here's what we found (with a free tool)". Engineering communities are the primary buyer and love self-serve tools.

### 4. Credex / consulting upsell
The "Book a free Credex consultation" CTA at the bottom of every audit result is the primary monetisation surface. Users who see a high overspend figure are warm leads for a managed AI procurement service.
